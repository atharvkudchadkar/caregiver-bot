Robot arm control

Stand comfortably with both controllers visible, then enter VR. Each controller sets its gripper's position relative to your headset, so lifting a controller lifts that gripper. The robot's head camera appears in the headset and the desktop window shows the MuJoCo scene.

Move the left thumbstick forward/back to drive. Move the right thumbstick sideways to turn. Hold either rear grip/squeeze button to close that hand's gripper; release to open it. Click either thumbstick to reset the gripper orientation reference.
