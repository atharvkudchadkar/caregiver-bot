Bracket Bot VR control

Stand comfortably with both controllers visible, then enter VR. Each controller sets its gripper's position relative to your headset, so lifting a controller lifts that gripper. The robot's head camera appears in the headset and the desktop window shows the MuJoCo scene.

Move the left thumbstick forward/back to drive. Turn your head left or right and the robot swivels through the same angle. Press either rear grip/squeeze button within 2 metres of the wheelchair to align with its orientation and attach both hands to its handles; press again to release. Click either thumbstick to reset the head and gripper orientation references.
